Support

Something broken, a question, or a request to delete your data — email support@topo.app. Replies usually come within a couple of days.

It helps to include what you were doing, what happened instead, and roughly when. If it is a display problem, the build number from Settings at the bottom of the app pins down exactly which version you are on.

Common questions

How do I delete my account?
In the app: Settings → Delete account. It takes effect immediately and removes your profile, connections, messages and the events you created. It cannot be undone.
Do you upload my contacts?
No. Each address is hashed on your phone and only the hashes are sent, to be compared against accounts that already exist. Nobody in your contacts is ever contacted, and the list is never stored.
Who can see my phone number or date of birth?
Nobody. Neither is shown to other people.
What does Nearby share?
Only while you switch it on, and it turns itself off after three hours. Other people with Nearby on see your first name, last initial and photo — your username stays hidden until you have both agreed to connect.
I forgot my password.
Use Forgot password? on the sign-in screen. Sign-in takes your username, not your email address.
