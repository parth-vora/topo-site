What it does

See how you know someone

Open anyone's profile and topo shows the connections you have in common, and the event where you met. It is the question a contacts app can never answer.

Events that remember

Host one or join with a code, and check in when you arrive. Afterwards topo shows the people who were there that you haven't added yet, ranked by mutual friends and shared interests — while you still remember the conversation.

Nearby

At an event, see who is actually in the room and connect in one tap. Nearby is off until you turn it on, turns itself off after three hours, and only runs while you are using it.

Private by design

Your contacts are hashed on your device and never uploaded. Your location is used only while Nearby is switched on. Your surname, date of birth, email and phone number are never shown to anyone.

Getting topo

topo is invite-only while it is new. If someone sent you an invite link, open it on your iPhone and it will take you to the right place.

Support

Something broken, a question, or a request to delete your data — email support@jointopo.app. Replies usually come within a couple of days.

It helps to include what you were doing, what happened instead, and roughly when. If it is a display problem, the build number at the bottom of Settings pins down exactly which version you are on.

Common questions

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Settings → Delete account. It takes effect immediately and removes your profile, connections, messages and the events you created. It cannot be undone.

Do you upload my contacts?

No. Each address is hashed on your phone with SHA-256 and only the hashes are sent, to be compared against accounts that already exist. A hash cannot be turned back into an address. Nobody in your contacts is ever contacted, and the list is never stored.

Who can see my phone number or date of birth?

Nobody. Neither is shown to other people.

What does Nearby share?

Only while you switch it on, and it turns itself off after three hours. Other people with Nearby on see your first name, last initial and photo — your username stays hidden until you have both agreed to connect.

I forgot my password.

Use Forgot password? on the sign-in screen. Sign-in takes your username, not your email address.
